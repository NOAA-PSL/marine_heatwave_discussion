Global Marine Heatwave Forecast Discussion - May 2026

Observed and forecasted values include the effects of long-term warming since 1990. Values with the long-term warming trend removed are in brackets.

Current marine heatwave conditions:        

In April 2026, 29% [18%] of the global ocean experienced marine heatwaves (MHWs), which ranked 20th [34th] among all months since 1991.

Currently, MHWs are present in the Eastern Tropical Pacific, along the North American west coast, in the Kuroshio Extension region, and in scattered regions of the Atlantic and Southern Oceans.

Marine heatwave forecasts:

Global MHW coverage is predicted to reach 40% [25%] by September 2026, with coverage of 40-45% [25-30%] persisting through the end of the year. Below is a regionally refined focus:

  • Tropical Pacific - With an expected El Niño event in Boreal winter, MHW likelihoods throughout the remainder of 2026 are very high in the Eastern Tropical Pacific (90-100% [80-100%]; high confidence) and low in the western Tropical Pacific (0-20% [0-10%]; medium confidence)
  • Northeast Pacific - High MHW likelihoods are forecast off the California and Baja California coasts through the end of the year (80-90% [60-80%]; medium confidence), with lower but still elevated likelihoods off Oregon and Washington (40-70% [30-50%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Central and Western North Pacific - Forecast MHWs probabilities across the central and western North Pacific decline but remain elevated through August (40-70% [30-50%]; low confidence).
  • Indian Ocean - MHW likelihoods are very high over much of the western Indian Ocean through August (70-100% [40-70%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Southern Ocean - Areas of high MHW probabilities over the next several months are scattered across all sectors of the Southern Ocean, with the highest likelihoods southeast of Africa and Australia (60-100% [40-90%]; low-medium confidence).
