Global Marine Heatwave Forecast Discussion - August 2026

Observed and forecasted values include the effects of long-term warming since 1990. Values with the long-term warming trend removed are in brackets.

Current marine heatwave conditions:        

In July 2026, 37% [27%] of the global ocean experienced marine heatwaves (MHWs), which ranked 2nd [2nd] among all months since 1991.

Currently, MHWs are present in the Eastern Pacific, the Sea of Japan and Kuroshio Extension region, the Subtropical North and Northeast Atlantic, the Greenland Sea, the Mediterranean Sea, the Tasman Sea, and scattered regions of the Southern Ocean.

Marine heatwave forecasts:

Global MHW coverage is predicted to reach 45% [>30%] in boreal winter 2026-2027, which would be the highest since at least 1991. Below is a regionally refined focus:

  • Tropical Pacific - With an ongoing El Niño event predicted to peak in boreal winter, MHW likelihoods throughout the remainder of 2026 are very high in the Eastern Tropical Pacific (90-100% [90-100%]; high confidence).
  • Northeast Pacific - High MHW likelihoods are forecast off the California and Baja California coasts through the end of the year (80-100% [70-100%]; high confidence), with lower but still elevated likelihoods off Oregon and Washington (30-70% [20-60%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Central and Western North Pacific - Forecast MHWs probabilities in the Kuroshio Extension region remain elevated through the end of the year (60-90% [30-70%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Northeast Atlantic - Continued high MHW likelihoods are predicted in the Northeast Atlantic through December (40-70% [10-50%]; low confidence).
  • Mediterranean Sea - MHW likelihoods in the Mediterranean are declining over the coming months, reaching 30-50% ([10-30%]; low confidence) in October.
  • Indian Ocean - MHW likelihoods are very high in the western and northern Indian Ocean through October (90-100% [80-100%]; medium confidence).
  • Tasman Sea - Continued MHW activity is likely in the Tasman Sea through November (60-90% [30-60%]; low confidence)
  • Southern Ocean - Areas of high MHW probabilities are scattered across all sectors of the Southern Ocean until the end of the year, with the highest likelihoods southwest of South America (70-100% [50-80%]; low-medium confidence) and southeast of Africa (70-100% [50-80%]; low confidence).
