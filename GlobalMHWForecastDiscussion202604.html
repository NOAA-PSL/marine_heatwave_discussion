Global Marine Heatwave Forecast Discussion - April 2026

Observed and forecasted values include the effects of long-term warming since 1990. Values with the long-term warming trend removed are in brackets.

Current marine heatwave conditions:        

In March 2026, 27% [16%] of the global ocean experienced marine heatwaves (MHWs), which ranked 23rd [44th] among all months since 1991.

Currently, MHWs are scattered across the western tropical Pacific, large portions of the extratropical Pacific, the extratropical Atlantic, the Southeast tropical Atlantic and Pacific, and the Southern Ocean.

Marine heatwave forecasts:

Global MHW coverage is predicted to continue rising over the coming year to ~40% [24%] by September 2026 before leveling off through the end of the year. Below is a regionally refined focus:

  • Tropical Pacific - MHW likelihoods are expected to increase through summer 2026 in the eastern tropical Pacific (80-100% [50-100%]; high confidence), and decrease in the western tropical Pacific (60-90% [30-50%]; low-medium confidence), consistent with the development of El Niño. By October 2026, widespread MHWs (80-100% [60-90%]; medium-high confidence) are expected across the eastern tropical Pacific.
  • Northeast Pacific - an ongoing MHW extending from Southern California to the coast of Baja California is expected to persist through at least September 2026 (60-90% [50-80%]; low-medium confidence). Long-lead forecasts suggest MHW conditions could persist and intensify, spreading along the North American west coast through fall/winter 2026 (60-70% [40-60%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Central and Western North Pacific - High probabilities of MHWs are forecast across the central and western North Pacific (60-80% [30-50%]; low confidence) at least through July.
  • North Atlantic - Elevated chances of MHWs are forecast through July over much of the Northwest Atlantic, particularly inside and east of the Gulf of Mexico (40-60% [10-30%]; low confidence) as well as in the seas surrounding Greenland (60-90% [50-80%]; low confidence).
  • Mediterranean Sea - MHW likelihoods in the Mediterranean are projected to remain elevated through July 2026 (40-70% [20-30%]; low confidence).
  • Southern Ocean - Areas of high MHW probabilities over the next several months are scattered across all sectors of the Southern Ocean, with the highest likelihoods southeast of Africa and Australia (60-100% [50-80%]; low-medium confidence).
