Global Marine Heatwave Forecast Discussion - November 2026

Observed and forecasted values include the effects of long-term warming since 1990. Values with the long-term warming trend removed are in brackets.

Current marine heatwave conditions:        

In October 2026, 42% [30%] of the global ocean experienced marine heatwaves (MHWs), which ranked 1st [1st] among all months since 1991.

Currently, MHWs are present in the Eastern Pacific, central North and Northwest Pacific, the Tropical and Subtropical Atlantic, the western Mediterranean Sea, the Indian Ocean, and scattered regions of the Arctic and Southern Oceans.

Marine heatwave forecasts:

Global MHW coverage has reached record levels as predicted. The latest forecasts predict even more expansion to approximately 44% [32%] in boreal winter 2026-2027, which would be the highest since at least 1991. Below is a regionally refined focus:

  • Tropical Pacific - With an ongoing very strong El Niño, MHW likelihoods exceeding 90% [90%] (high confidence) are expected in the Eastern Tropical Pacific through boreal winter.
  • Northeast Pacific - High MHW likelihoods are forecast off the coasts of California and Baja California (80-100% [80-100%]; high confidence) as well as Oregon and Washington (60-80% [30-80%]; low-medium confidence) through winter.
  • Central and Western North Pacific - Forecast MHW probabilities in the East China Sea, Sea of Japan, and Kuroshio Extension region remain elevated into January 2027 (80-100% [40-80%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Atlantic - High MHW likelihoods are predicted in the tropical Atlantic through January, particularly in the vicinity of the Caribbean (90-100% [80-100%]; medium-high confidence) and east of Brazil (90-100% [80-100%]; medium confidence).
  • Mediterranean Sea - Elevated MHW likelihoods are shifting from the western to central Mediterranean over the coming months, reaching 50-70% ([10-40%]; low confidence) in January.
  • Indian Ocean and Coral Triangle - Through early 2027, MHW likelihoods are very high throughout the Indian Ocean and Coral Triangle (90-100% [60-90%]; medium-high confidence).
  • Arctic Ocean - High MHW likelihoods are forecast through January in the Norwegian and Barents Seas (60-100% [40-90%]; low-medium confidence).
