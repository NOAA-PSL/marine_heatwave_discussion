Global Marine Heatwave Forecast Discussion - September 2026

Observed and forecasted values include the effects of long-term warming since 1990. Values with the long-term warming trend removed are in brackets.

Current marine heatwave conditions:        

In August 2026, 38% [27%] of the global ocean experienced marine heatwaves (MHWs), which ranked 2nd [1st] among all months since 1991.

Currently, MHWs are present in the Eastern Pacific, central North Pacific, the Subtropical North and Northeast Atlantic, the Greenland Sea, the Mediterranean Sea, the western Indian Ocean, and scattered regions of the Southern Ocean.

Marine heatwave forecasts:

Global MHW coverage is predicted to reach 45% [>30%] in boreal winter 2026-2027, which would be the highest since at least 1991. Below is a regionally refined focus:

  • Tropical Pacific - With an ongoing strong El Niño, MHW likelihoods exceeding 90% [90%] (high confidence) are expected in the Eastern Tropical Pacific through boreal winter 2026-27.
  • Northeast Pacific - High MHW likelihoods are forecast off the California and Baja California coasts through the end of the year (80-100% [70-100%]; high confidence), with lower but still elevated likelihoods off Oregon and Washington (40-80% [20-60%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Central and Western North Pacific - Forecast MHWs probabilities in the Kuroshio Extension region remain elevated through the end of the year (50-100% [30-80%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Atlantic - High MHW likelihoods are predicted in the Subtropical North and South Atlantic through the end of the year, particularly in the vicinity of the Caribbean (80-100% [60-80%]; medium-high confidence) and east of Brazil (40-70% [70-100%]; low confidence).
  • Mediterranean Sea - MHW likelihoods in the Mediterranean are declining over the coming months, reaching 20-60% ([0-30%]; low confidence) in November.
  • Indian Ocean - MHW likelihoods are very high in the western and northern Indian Ocean through December (90-100% [70-100%]; medium confidence).
  • Southern Ocean - Areas of high MHW probabilities are scattered across all sectors of the Southern Ocean until the end of the year, with the highest likelihoods southwest of South America (80-100% [70-100%]; low-medium confidence) and south of Australia (80-100% [60-80%]; low-medium confidence).
