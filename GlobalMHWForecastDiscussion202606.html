Global Marine Heatwave Forecast Discussion - June 2026

Observed and forecasted values include the effects of long-term warming since 1990. Values with the long-term warming trend removed are in brackets.

Current marine heatwave conditions:        

In May 2026, 28% [18%] of the global ocean experienced marine heatwaves (MHWs), which ranked 24th [36th] among all months since 1991.

Currently, MHWs are present in the Eastern Pacific (especially in the tropics), the Kuroshio Extension region, the Mediterranean Sea, and in scattered regions of the Atlantic and Southern Oceans.

Marine heatwave forecasts:

Global MHW coverage is predicted to exceed 40% [30%] by boreal winter 2025-2026. Below is a regionally refined focus:

  • Tropical Pacific - With an ongoing El Niño event predicted to peak in boreal winter, MHW likelihoods throughout the remainder of 2026 are very high in the Eastern Tropical Pacific (90-100% [90-100%]; high confidence) and low in the western Tropical Pacific (0-30% [0-20%]; low-medium confidence)
  • Northeast Pacific - High MHW likelihoods are forecast off the California and Baja California coasts through the end of the year (80-100% [70-90%]; medium-high confidence), with lower but still elevated likelihoods off Oregon and Washington (40-70% [30-60%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Central and Western North Pacific - Forecast MHWs probabilities in the Kuroshio Extension region remain elevated through September (40-80% [20-40%]; low confidence).
  • Mediterranean Sea - MHW likelihoods in the Mediterranean are elevated but declining over the coming months, reaching 40-60% ([10-30%]; low confidence) in August.
  • Indian Ocean - MHW likelihoods are very high in the southwestern Indian Ocean through September (90-100% [70-100%]; low-medium confidence).
  • Southern Ocean - Areas of high MHW probabilities over the next several months are scattered across all sectors of the Southern Ocean, with the highest likelihoods southeast of Africa and Australia (80-100% [50-100%]; low-medium confidence).
